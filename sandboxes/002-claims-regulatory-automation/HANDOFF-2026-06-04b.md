### Smell 5 Resolution

The Smell 5 (Regulatory Mapping) zero-findings problem is resolved. The detector was rebuilt as a two-tier system:

- **H001-H003** (lexical): retained; produce zero hits on current carrier corpus but kept for completeness.
- **H004** (graph gap, MEDIUM): carrier rate-setting claim with no regulatory edge — 5 source-level findings
- **H005** (graph gap, MEDIUM): carrier mandatory-coverage claim with no regulatory edge — 2 source-level findings
- **H006** (graph gap, LOW): carrier loss-settlement methodology with no regulatory edge — 5 source-level findings

H004-H006 emit one consolidated finding per source with `supporting_nodes` list preserving all triggering nodes.

### Stage 005 Architectural Finding

Stage 005 semantic retrieval is complete with a key architectural conclusion: **vector similarity cannot detect absence**. Smell 5 is a gap smell — carrier nodes that lack traceable regulatory links. Both `text-embedding-3-small` and `text-embedding-3-large` failed to surface the five Smell 5 paraphrase gold set items. The correct architecture is graph-based gap detection using the existing Stage 002 edge substrate. See ADR-010.

Vector store selection (ADR-005 equivalent) remains deferred. Vector retrieval earns a future role in cross-carrier paraphrase matching, not in gap detection.
