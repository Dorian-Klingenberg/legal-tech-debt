## Critical Product Principles (Read Before Any Product Work)

### 1. Ground truth is internal disclosure, not external comparison
The product audits a SERFF filing package as an isolated graph. Every reference must resolve to a payload; every defined term must have a definition; every endorsed coverage must have a form. We do not compare against ISO to make findings — we check the carrier's own documents for completeness.

### 2. ISO as implicit gold standard — no copy needed
ISO HO 00 03/05 content is established fact in insurance regulation and litigation. SERFF searches confirmed that neither KNIC nor KFBM filed ISO base forms independently (KNIC licenses by reference; KFBM uses proprietary HO 04 93). BACKLOG-018 closed. See ADR-011.

### 3. Two distinct products
- **Phase 1 (built):** Gaps in the carrier's own filed documents. Self-contained. All three entries are Phase 1.
- **Phase 2 (future):** How the carrier diverged from ISO and the risk profile of each change. Requires ISO base forms. Different product, higher build cost.

### 4. Target carriers are ISO divergers
Pure ISO adopters are low-value targets. Legal tech debt lives in: proprietary base forms (KFBM HO 04 93), heavy endorsement stacks, and proprietary rate/underwriting methodologies. KFBM is the higher-value corpus target of the two carriers analyzed.

### 5. Four structural gap types (Jem framework)
1. **Phantom Form** — form referenced in schedule but PDF payload missing (already implemented: Smell 5/ADR-010)
2. **Broken Definitions Loop** — defined term used in body but absent from Definitions Section (partial: Smell 2 H003)
3. **Undisclosed Rating Rules vs. Policy Constraints** — Rule Manual describes endorsement; no form in attachment list (partial: Smell 4/5)
4. **Missing State Amendatory** — multi-state master jacket without required state amendatory (new: BACKLOG-019)
