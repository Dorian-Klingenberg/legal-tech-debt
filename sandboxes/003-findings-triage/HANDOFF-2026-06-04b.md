## Product Model (Important Context for Next Agent)

**Executive summary** (`executive_summary.md`) = sales instrument. Gets a CEO/CCO to the table. Pattern-level, no filing detail. Generated once per carrier cohort.

**Expert drill-down report** (BACKLOG-017) = the paid service. Carrier-specific, finding-specific, actionable. Three reader roles:
- **Compliance officer / coverage attorney** — regulatory grounding, exact citations, defensible suggested language
- **Claims professional** — dispute exposure per finding, bad-faith risk framing
- **Policy designer / filing specialist** — proposed redlines or remediation templates, DOI refiling guidance

Suggested fix strategy by smell type: Smell 4/5 → concrete redline (add citation/version). Smell 2 → remediation template (methodology is carrier-specific, can't prescribe a single fix).
